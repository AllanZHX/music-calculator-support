3. Performance recording and M4A export

Music Calculator does not request or use microphone access. It does not capture room audio, conversations, or ambient sound. A “recording” is a timed record of notes and performance key events.

Only when you explicitly export a work does the App generate an M4A file on your device from those events and open the system save or share interface. The file is then handled by the Files location, cloud service, messaging tool, or other third-party app you choose, under that recipient's policy.

4. Google advertising and consent

The free version integrates Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to load app-open and interstitial ads and manage applicable privacy choices. Depending on your region, device settings, consent, and ad type, Google and its partners may process:

  • IP address and coarse location inferred from it;
  • device identifiers, advertising identifiers, and device information where permission and law allow;
  • ad impressions, clicks, interactions, product interaction, and frequency information;
  • crash logs, diagnostics, and performance data; and
  • information used for ad delivery, measurement, frequency control, fraud prevention, security, and consent management.

Where applicable, the App may request Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) authorization. Refusing ATT or personalized advertising does not block calculation, performance, scores, or local saving. You may still receive non-personalized, contextual, or restricted ads. Permanent Unlock removes ads from the App.

See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from partner apps.

5. Apple services, purchases, and ratings

Apple processes App Store delivery, StoreKit purchases, payments, billing, refunds, transaction revocations, and system rating prompts. The App may receive a product identifier, transaction verification result, and Permanent Unlock entitlement status to deliver purchased features. The developer does not receive your full payment-card information or Apple Account password.

The system rating prompt is provided by Apple. The App records launch and prompt state locally, but cannot determine whether the system prompt appeared, whether you submitted a rating, or what rating you gave. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Purposes and legal bases

Depending on the law that applies where you live, processing described in this policy may rely on:

  • Performance of a contract: providing calculation, music, saving, export, and purchased features;
  • Your consent: personalized advertising, tracking, or other processing that requires consent; withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal;
  • Legitimate interests: security, fraud prevention, diagnostics, reliability, and general operations where those interests do not override your rights and freedoms; and
  • Legal obligations: valid legal requests, transaction records, and regulatory duties.

7. Sharing and international transfers

The developer does not operate a server that receives your calculation history, scores, or saved performances and does not sell this local content. Information connected with App functions may be handled by:

  • Apple for App Store distribution, purchases, refunds, and system services;
  • Google and its advertising partners for ads, measurement, consent, security, and fraud prevention; and
  • Destinations you choose when saving or sharing an M4A to a file location, cloud service, or third-party app.

Apple and Google may process information outside your country or region and use contractual terms, certifications, standard clauses, or other lawful safeguards that apply to them. Their published policies describe their locations and mechanisms in more detail.

8. Retention

On-device data remains until you delete it in the App, reset the relevant preference, or uninstall the App. An exported file remains until you delete it from its destination. Purchase records, advertising information, diagnostics, and other data controlled by Apple, Google, or a sharing destination are retained under their policies for service delivery, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, and legal obligations.

9. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, receive portable data, withdraw consent, or opt out of certain sale, sharing, or targeted advertising. You can:

  • delete calculation history, custom scores, and saved performances in the App;
  • uninstall the App to clear its local container;
  • reopen the App's privacy options when that entry is available;
  • manage ATT permission in iOS or iPadOS Settings;
  • contact Apple or Google through their privacy channels for data they control; and
  • email the developer with other requests or complaints available under applicable law.

For information the developer never receives and that exists only on your device, the developer cannot remotely retrieve or erase it; use the App's deletion controls or uninstall the App. We may reasonably verify a requester's identity before responding. You may also complain to a competent privacy authority in your location.

10. Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal information directly to the developer. If you believe a child sent personal information to the developer without appropriate parental or guardian consent, contact us and we will take reasonable steps based on the available information and applicable law.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures proportionate to the size of the App, the information involved, and the way it is processed. These measures include keeping creative content on the device where practical, using Apple transaction verification, and limiting unnecessary processing. No device, network, storage, or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure, so protect your device and exported files.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when functions, laws, or third-party services change. The version and “Last updated” date on this page will change. For updates that may materially affect your rights, we will try to provide a prominent notice in the App or on the website and obtain renewed consent when required by law. Please review this page periodically.
