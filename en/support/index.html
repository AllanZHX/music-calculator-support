Quick help

What should I do if calculator keys make no sound?

Turn up the media volume on your iPhone or iPad and check whether audio is being routed to Bluetooth headphones, a speaker, or another device. Return to the App and press a key again. If needed, disconnect the external audio device and restart the App.

Why does my score contain unsupported notes?

Music Calculator provides Low, Middle, and High key-pitch presets. If one or more notes in a custom numbered score fall outside the selected preset, the App reports that they cannot be covered. Try a different pitch preset or simplify the out-of-range notes.

What if playback or the last note does not work correctly?

Leave the current song, reopen it, and try again. If the issue continues, email the song name, exact steps, device model, system version, and App version so the problem can be reproduced.

Does a performance recording capture room audio?

No. Music Calculator records performance key events and their timing; it does not use the microphone and does not capture conversations or ambient sound. You choose whether to start recording from Performance mode.

When is an M4A file created?

The App creates an M4A on your device only when you explicitly export a saved performance. The free version allows one successful M4A export per day. Permanent Unlock removes this limit. The exported file is then controlled by the Files location or sharing destination you choose.

Purchases, restoration, and refunds

How do I restore a purchase?

Sign in with the same Apple Account used for the original purchase. Open the Permanent Unlock screen in the App and select Restore Purchases. Apple verifies the transaction; the developer does not receive your card details or Apple Account password.

What if I paid but the limits remain?

Keep the device online, reopen the Permanent Unlock screen, and select Restore Purchases. If access is still not restored, send the purchase time, device model, and a screenshot of the issue. Do not send a full receipt identifier, password, or payment-card information.

How do I request an Apple refund?

Apple handles payment, billing, and refunds. Visit Apple's Report a Problem page to request an Apple refund. A refunded, revoked, or invalidated transaction may end the related Permanent Unlock entitlement.

Ads and privacy options

The free version may show app-open and interstitial ads. Depending on your region, Google User Messaging Platform provides applicable privacy choices, and the App may request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) authorization. Refusing tracking does not block calculation, music, scores, or local saving; you may still receive non-personalized, contextual, or restricted ads. Permanent Unlock removes ads.
